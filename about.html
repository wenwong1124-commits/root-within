Meet your therapist

Karina Cheung MSW, RSW

Social Worker | Psychotherapist

I am a Registered Social Worker who sees therapy as a safe space to reconnect with yourself.

My work is grounded in the belief that each person's experiences are unique, and that meaningful change can begin when we have a compassionate space to explore what is happening within and around us.

I have a particular interest in supporting individuals navigating

grief, loss, and bereavement

This interest is informed in part by my own experience of navigating grief in a cultural context where conversations about death and dying may be difficult.

I also support individuals experiencing

  • anxiety
  • depression
  • stress and burnout
  • emotional regulation difficulties
  • communication and relationship challenges
  • autism and ADHD
  • life transitions
  • caregiver burnout

My approach is

  • holistic
  • person-centered
  • empowering

I strive to create a warm and welcoming environment where you can explore your emotions, thoughts, and lived experiences at your own pace. I tailor my approach to your unique needs, strengths, and circumstances, recognizing that there is no one-size-fits-all approach to therapy.
